Paper Wings

Project Info:

This project is an exploration into frame-by-frame animation using the program Krita. It started out as a demonstration of the steps into pose to pose animation. I started with a guide line of the path I wanted the arrow to follow, then I drew in the key points. It was at that point I decided to make it into a paper airplane to practice different principals of animation and even work on perspective. From there I added the frames in between and refined the shapes.
