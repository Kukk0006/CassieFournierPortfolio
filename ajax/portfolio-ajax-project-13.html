Coco Yang YouTube

Project Info:

In this project, I collaborated with Coco Yang to enhance her YouTube channel, Coco Creates Art, by animating her logo based on her established branding guide. Using Krita’s animation tools, I crafted a frame-by-frame animation of the snake and sword, ensuring that the motion aligned with the dynamic style of her channel. After creating the animation with an alpha background, I transitioned to After Effects to integrate the final elements, including text and a background design, adding depth and cohesiveness to the overall composition.

This was my first venture into hand-drawn animation, which allowed me to experience the meticulous process of frame-by-frame creation. The hands-on nature of this approach taught me valuable lessons in timing and motion that I hadn’t encountered in other projects, making this experience particularly rewarding.
